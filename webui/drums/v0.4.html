Key bindings:
  • SPACE: play or stop
  • P: pause
  • T: tap to tempo
  • M: mute
  • F: toggle fullscreen (ESC also exits)
  • N (or R): next riff
  • V: vary the riff
  • [ and ]: rotate the riff back / forward one beat (wrapping around)
  • -: decrease tempo
  • +: increase tempo
Riff pane: a one-note rhythm riff that plays in sync with the drums. It is drawn centered on its own scale (large when it is a single bar), and lines up with the drum columns when it is as long as the drum pattern. Each black box is a beat; the number under it is how many slots it is split into (1 quarter, 2 eighths, 3 triplet, 4 sixteenths, 5 quintuplet, 6 sextuplet; in 6/8: 1, 3, 6) — click the number to cycle it. Click a slot to cycle rest → note → accent (>) → tie → rest; shift-click switches a note between staccato and long. A tie (offered only when the slot before it is voiced) carries the previous note on through that slot, including across a beat boundary. The Riff text uses one character per slot (. rest, x staccato, X accent, o long, O accented long, - tie), beats separated by commas, bars by |. The Chords field is a simple progression, one chord per play of the riff: E E A A plays the riff twice on E and twice on A, then repeats. Only the root matters (Am7 is A), pitched in the octave nearest the selected note; the box for the chord being played lights up. It is carried in the riff text as ; chords=…. New rolls a fresh riff from the generator settings (the 🎛️ button next to New opens them); Vary makes a small change; Auto does a Vary every N plays, so it drifts toward wherever the settings are. The 📜 dropdown next to New holds the last 25 riffs, newest first: a riff goes in once it has played through unchanged, whether it came from New, Vary, Auto or your own edits.
